Start here

If your skin changed and your old routine stopped working, start here.

I'm Katherine. I'm 48, I'm a nurse, and somewhere in my 40s the routine that had worked for twenty years quietly stopped working. I had a drawer of half-used serums to show for it. What finally helped was not another product but a plan: knowing what to do first, what to add later, and what I could skip entirely. This page is that plan, pieced together from what I have read, what I have tried on my own skin, and what I have learned along the way.

The plan

The order I would do things in

If I were starting over today, this is the sequence I would follow. Each step builds on the one before it, and most of the visible change in my own skin came from getting the first three right.

  1. 01

    Protect your skin every morning

    A broad-spectrum sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, used every day of the year. Everything else on this list works better when UV is not undoing it overnight.

    Mineral vs chemical sunscreen guide →
  2. 02

    Support your barrier

    A gentle cleanser and a moisturizer your skin likes. Midlife skin tends to get drier and more reactive, and a calm barrier makes every active you add later easier to tolerate.

    My 5-step morning routine →
  3. 03

    Add a retinoid, slowly

    Retinoids have the strongest evidence of any topical for fine lines and texture. Start two or three nights a week and let your skin set the pace from there.

    How to start retinol →
  4. 04

    Add one targeted active for your main concern

    Pick the thing that bothers you most, whether that is dark spots, dullness, or redness, and add one ingredient aimed at it rather than five at once.

    Ingredients worth paying for after 45 →
  5. 05

    Then consider devices and in-office treatments

    Once the basics are steady, tools like LED and microcurrent or treatments like peels and microneedling can build on them. They tend to work best on skin that is already well cared for.

    At-home devices → The peel menu →
