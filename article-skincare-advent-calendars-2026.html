Before you buy

How to pick the right one

Look at the full sizes, not just the value. A big "worth" number can come from lots of tiny samples. Calendars like Dermstore and Liberty, where many of the products are full size, give you more to use.

Match it to her routine. If she loves skin care, pick one heavy on serums and moisturizers. If she loves makeup, hair and scent, a mix like Bluemercury or Charlotte Tilbury is more fun.

Go slow with strong actives. Calendars often include retinoids, acids and vitamin C. Try one new active at a time, and keep retinoids and exfoliating acids on different nights. My retinol guide walks through how to start.

Buy early. The most popular calendars often sell out well before December 1.

Common questions

Which skincare advent calendar is the best value in 2026?

For anti-aging skin care, the Best of Dermstore 24 Days of Beauty calendar is $250 for products worth $1,147, and about half of them are full size. Liberty is also strong value, with 21 full-size products out of 29.

Is there a good skincare advent calendar under $50?

Yes. Target's 12-piece Skincare Advent Gift Set is about $20 and is filled with pharmacy brands like La Roche-Posay, Avène and CeraVe.

When do skincare advent calendars sell out?

The most popular calendars often sell out in November, well before December 1. If you have your eye on one, it is worth buying early.

Are advent calendars good for sensitive skin?

Calendars mix many brands and ingredients, so if your skin is sensitive, patch test each new product and introduce strong actives like retinoids slowly, one at a time.

This is general education from my own research and routine, not medical advice for your skin. If you use prescription skincare or have a skin condition, check with your dermatologist before adding new products.
