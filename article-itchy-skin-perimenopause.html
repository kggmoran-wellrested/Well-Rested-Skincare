Itchy skin in perimenopause: why it happens and what helps

Nobody warns you about the itching. Here is why your skin suddenly feels tight, dry and itchy, what dermatologists suggest, and what I would put on it.

By Katherine
November 2026
7 min read

Hot flashes get all the attention. Itching is the perimenopause symptom women tend to find out about on their own, usually in winter, usually at night, and usually after a hot shower. Shins, arms, back, sometimes the face. Skin that never gave you trouble starts to feel a size too small.

I am a nurse, and at 48 my own skin is drier than it has ever been. This is how I think about itchy skin at this stage: what is going on, what to change first, what to put on it, and when to stop treating it at home and call a doctor.

Why perimenopause makes skin itch

Estrogen does a lot of quiet work in the skin. As it falls, skin makes less oil and holds on to less water, and it can become thinner and more easily irritated. Dry skin itches. Skin that is dry and thin also reacts to things it used to ignore, like a wool sweater, a scented body wash or a new detergent.

Then winter piles on. Indoor heat dries the air, showers get hotter and longer, and the itch that was a small annoyance in October keeps you up in January.

When to call a doctor

Dry skin is the most common reason for itching at this age, but it is far from the only one. See a doctor if the itch lasts more than two weeks, keeps you awake, covers your whole body, or comes with a rash or other new symptoms. The American Academy of Dermatology says the same about itch that does not improve with home care. Itching in the vaginal area is a separate conversation for your gynecologist.

Change the shower before you buy anything

These come from dermatologists' guidance on itchy skin, and they cost nothing.

  • Lukewarm, and under 10 minutesHot water feels wonderful on itchy skin and makes it worse afterward.
  • Fragrance free everythingWash, lotion and laundry detergent. Look for the words "fragrance free". "Unscented" can still contain fragrance that has been masked.
  • Moisturize on damp skinPat dry, then apply cream within a few minutes while skin is still a little damp.
  • Loose cottonTight seams and scratchy fabrics keep the itch going.
  • A humidifier in winterEspecially in the bedroom.
  • A cool cloth for the bad spotsFive to ten minutes with a cool, wet cloth calms an itchy patch. Keeping your moisturizer in the refrigerator gives the same cooling effect.

When it is your face

An itchy, stinging face is usually a sign to do less. Put the retinoid, the acids and the vitamin C away for a couple of weeks and use three things: a gentle cleanser, a barrier cream and a mineral sunscreen. Bring the actives back one at a time once your skin feels comfortable.

  • La Roche-Posay Toleriane Moisturizing Milky Facial CleanserA milky cleanser that does not foam or strip.
  • Aestura Atobarrier 365 CreamA ceramide barrier cream for skin that feels tight and reactive.
  • Avène Cicalfate+ Hydrating Skin Recovery EmulsionA lighter recovery moisturizer if creams feel like too much during the day.
  • Avène Thermal Spring WaterA plain mist for the moments your face feels hot and prickly.

What to put away for now

  • Scrubs, loofahs and exfoliating glovesThey feel like they are helping the itch. They are making the barrier worse.
  • Exfoliating acids and body retinolGood for rough, crepey skin, and too much for skin that is itchy. They can come back later.
  • Scented body oils and washesFragrance is one of the most common irritants in skin care.
  • Long, hot bathsSave them for when your skin has calmed down, and keep them short.

When skin itches, the answer is almost never one more product. It is fewer, plainer ones.

The bottom line

Itchy skin in perimenopause is common, and for most women it starts with dryness. Cool the shower down, go fragrance free, moisturize on damp skin, and put your actives away until things settle. Give that two weeks. If the itch is still there, or it is keeping you up at night, see a doctor so they can find the cause.
